## Key Skills for Physical Design Engineers

### Essential Technical Skills
1. **EDA Tools Proficiency**
   - Cadence: Encounter/Innovus, Tempus, Genus
   - Synopsys: ICC/ICC2, PrimeTime, Design Compiler
   - Mentor Graphics: Calibre

2. **Physical Design Knowledge**
   - RTL to GDSII flow
   - Floorplanning and power planning
   - Placement and routing
   - Clock tree synthesis
   - Timing closure and optimization
   - Physical verification (DRC, LVS, ERC)

3. **Programming & Scripting**
   - TCL (essential for EDA tools)
   - Perl/Python for automation
   - Verilog/VHDL for RTL understanding
   - UNIX/Linux environment

4. **Process Technology Understanding**
   - Deep submicron effects
   - FinFET technology (7nm, 5nm)
   - Low-power design techniques
   - Multi-corner, multi-mode analysis

### Important Soft Skills
- Problem-solving and debugging
- Attention to detail
- Team collaboration
- Communication skills
- Time management

## Resume Writing Tips for Beginners

### 1. Format and Structure
- Keep to 1-2 pages maximum
- Use consistent formatting
- Clear section headings
- Professional font (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
- Reverse chronological order

### 2. Content Strategy
- Lead with education since you're a fresh graduate
- Transform academic projects into professional experiences
- Use action verbs (Designed, Implemented, Optimized, Achieved)
- Quantify achievements where possible

### 3. Tailoring for Physical Design Roles
- Research the specific company and role
- Match keywords from job descriptions
- Highlight relevant coursework and projects
- Show progression in technical complexity

### 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Don't oversell limited experience
- Avoid generic objectives
- Don't list irrelevant work experience prominently
- Ensure technical accuracy in tool names and processes

## Industry Keywords and Buzzwords

### Must-Have Keywords for Physical Design Resumes:
- **Flow Keywords**: RTL-to-GDSII, Front-end design, Back-end design, Signoff
- **Tools**: Innovus, ICC2, PrimeTime, Calibre, Encounter, Design Compiler
- **Processes**: Synthesis, Floorplanning, Placement, Routing, CTS, STA
- **Technologies**: 7nm, 14nm, 28nm, FinFET, SOI, Multi-Vt
- **Verification**: DRC, LVS, ERC, Physical verification, Tape-out
- **Analysis**: Timing analysis, Power analysis, SI analysis, IR drop
- **Scripting**: TCL, Perl, Python, SKILL

### Industry Buzzwords:
- Deep submicron design
- Multi-corner multi-mode (MCMM)
- Clock domain crossing (CDC)
- Design for testability (DFT)
- Power integrity
- Signal integrity
- Electromigration
- Lithography-friendly design
