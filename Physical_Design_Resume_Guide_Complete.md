## SECTION 2: RESUME WRITING TIPS & BEST PRACTICES

### Format and Structure Guidelines

**1. Overall Layout:**
- Length: 1-2 pages maximum (1 page preferred for entry-level)
- Font: Professional fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, 10-12pt)
- Margins: 0.5-1 inch on all sides
- Spacing: Consistent line spacing (1.0-1.15)
- File Format: PDF preferred (preserves formatting)

**2. Section Order for Fresh Graduates:**
1. Contact Information
2. Professional Summary/Objective (2-3 lines)
3. Education (prominent placement)
4. Technical Skills
5. Academic Projects (detailed)
6. Experience (if any)
7. Certifications/Achievements

**3. Professional Contact Information:**
```
Full Name (larger font, bold)
Phone Number | Professional Email Address
LinkedIn Profile URL | Location (City, State)
```

**4. Formatting Best Practices:**
- Use bullet points (•) for easy scanning
- Bold section headers and job titles
- Consistent date formatting (Month Year - Month Year)
- Left-align all text (avoid center alignment)
- Use action verbs to start bullet points
- Maintain consistent indentation

### Content Strategy for Beginners

**1. Professional Summary Guidelines:**
- Keep to 2-3 lines maximum
- Mention your degree and specialization
- Highlight 1-2 key technical strengths
- Include career objective/target role
- Avoid generic statements

Example:
"Recent Electrical Engineering graduate specializing in VLSI Physical Design with hands-on experience in RTL-to-GDSII implementation using industry-standard EDA tools. Seeking entry-level physical design engineer position to apply expertise in timing optimization and physical verification."

**2. Education Section Strategy:**
- Lead with highest degree
- Include GPA if 3.5 or higher
- List relevant coursework (4-6 courses max)
- Mention thesis/capstone project
- Include academic honors/awards

**3. Academic Projects as Professional Experience:**
Transform academic work using professional language:

Instead of: "Did a project on processor design"
Write: "Implemented ARM Cortex-M0 processor physical design achieving 800MHz timing closure with 15% area optimization"

**4. Quantifying Achievements:**
Always include metrics when possible:
- Timing improvements (% faster, MHz achieved)
- Area reduction (% smaller, mm² savings)
- Power optimization (% reduction, mW savings)
- Tool efficiency (% faster flow, hours saved)

**5. Technical Skills Organization:**
Group skills by category for easy scanning:
- EDA Tools (Implementation, Analysis, Verification)
- Programming Languages (by proficiency level)
- Design Methodologies
- Process Technologies

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

**1. Content Mistakes:**
- ❌ Using generic objective statements
- ❌ Listing responsibilities instead of achievements
- ❌ Including irrelevant work experience prominently
- ❌ Overselling limited experience
- ❌ Using abbreviations without explanation
- ❌ Inconsistent technical terminology

**2. Formatting Mistakes:**
- ❌ Inconsistent fonts, sizes, or spacing
- ❌ Poor bullet point alignment
- ❌ Crowded layout with no white space
- ❌ Using tables or graphics (ATS unfriendly)
- ❌ Inconsistent date formats
- ❌ Missing contact information

**3. Technical Mistakes:**
- ❌ Incorrect tool names (e.g., "ICC" instead of "ICC2")
- ❌ Misusing technical terms
- ❌ Claiming expertise in unfamiliar tools
- ❌ Inconsistent technology node references
- ❌ Mixing analog and digital design terms incorrectly
